Hi! I’m Noah Solomon.

I am an aspiring hardware and software engineer, with experience ranging from user applications all the way down to the HDL level. I have produced multiple reliable applications; I developed new photon emission microscope control software and used it for FPGA security research, and I overhauled the digital circuits course at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI), introducing a cross-platform Verilator-based interactive simulator.

I’m interested in many aspects of both CS and ECE, especially relating to how computers work at various levels. For instance, I love learning about programming language theory/design and compilers and have lately spent a lot of time reading Python development discussions, and I am also fascinated by hardware security research and digital design. I am passionate about making tools and user interfaces that are accessible to people with all levels of computer skills.

I recently graduated from Worcester Polytechnic Institute with a Bachelor’s in Computer Science and Electrical & Computer Engineering. I am based in Seattle and seeking employment in the region. Here is my current resume.

Projects
